## `01 // About Me`

Hi, I'm **Liu Yang (刘扬)**, a robotics researcher and engineering student at **Zhejiang University** with a background in **Automation** from **Ocean University of China**. I work at the intersection of **robot learning, motion control, and autonomous navigation**.

My work spans two complementary directions: teaching humanoid robots to move robustly in complex environments, and enabling heterogeneous robot teams to perceive, plan, and navigate cooperatively.

- 🤖 **Research focus:** Humanoid locomotion, reinforcement learning, embodied intelligence, and vision-language navigation (VLN).
- 🧠 **Current interests:** Whole-body control, behavior representations, motion priors, foundation models for control, and sim-to-real generalization.
- 🛰️ **Systems background:** UAV–UGV cooperative navigation, SLAM, topological reasoning, and learning-based task scheduling.
- ⚙️ **Engineering mindset:** From algorithm design and simulation to implementation, benchmarking, and deployment-oriented validation.
- 🧩 **Community / team:** `RoboParty_Lab` · [RoboParty GitHub Organization](https://github.com/Roboparty).

> **Research philosophy:** Learn generalizable behaviors. Build reliable systems. Validate beyond simulation.

## `03 // Selected Research & Engineering Projects`

### 🛰️ Topology-Aware Learning for Air–Ground Cooperative Navigation

**Project lead / first author · Zhejiang University FAST Lab · Dec 2025 – Jun 2026**

Designed a cooperative navigation framework in which **one perception-equipped UAV** supports **multiple perception-limited UGVs** through shared mapping and intelligent aerial task scheduling.

- Developed a **reinforcement-learning-based UAV scheduling strategy** to deliver relevant local map information to UGVs on demand.
- Designed **GVD-based topological analysis** to identify dead ends in advance, mark problematic entrances, and guide UGVs away from navigation traps.
- Integrated local guidance generation with ground-robot planning to reduce unnecessary detours and improve navigation efficiency.
- Completed simulation-stage development; the résumé records hardware validation and manuscript preparation as subsequent work.

`Multi-Robot Systems` `Reinforcement Learning` `SLAM` `GVD` `Topological Planning` `UAV–UGV`

### 🦿 Perceptive Humanoid Locomotion on Uneven Terrain

**Research contributor · Nov 2025 – Feb 2026**

Participated in learning-based locomotion research using a **humanoid robot platform** for non-flat terrain traversal.

- Incorporated **LiDAR-derived elevation maps** into the locomotion policy's perceptive observations.
- Worked within a **teacher–student reinforcement learning / policy distillation** framework.
- Contributed to **reward-function design and tuning**, while exploring curriculum learning and domain randomization for robust transfer.
- Gained practical experience with **Isaac Gym / Isaac Lab** and legged-robot reinforcement learning workflows.

`Humanoid Robotics` `Perceptive Locomotion` `Isaac Lab` `RL` `Policy Distillation` `Sim-to-Real`

### 🔬 Deep Learning for Industrial Surface Defect Detection

**Undergraduate thesis · Nov 2024 – May 2025**

Developed a surface-defect detection pipeline for industrial aluminum materials, emphasizing data scarcity and small-defect recognition.

- Used **DCGAN** to augment limited and imbalanced training samples.
- Improved a **YOLOv8**-based detector with **SPD-Conv** and **SE Attention** components.
- Evaluated the approach on **NEU-DET**, **GC10-DET**, and real industrial samples.
- The thesis received an **Outstanding Undergraduate Thesis** distinction at Ocean University of China; the solution was used in an industrial inspection system.

`Computer Vision` `PyTorch` `YOLOv8` `DCGAN` `Object Detection` `Industrial AI`
